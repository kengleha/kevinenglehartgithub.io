# Dr. Kevin Englehart, PhD, P.Eng, FCAE, FIEEE

**Professor**, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering  
**Institute of Biomedical Engineering**, University of New Brunswick  

---

## About & Research Overview
Dr. Kevin Englehart's research focuses on the intersection of **biomedical signal processing**, **pattern recognition**, and **neurorehabilitation**. His work has significantly advanced the state of the art in intuitive, real-time electromyographic (EMG) control schemes for powered upper-limb prostheses and human-machine interfaces.

### Research Themes
* **Myoelectric Control & Deep Learning:** CNNs and time-frequency feature extraction for simultaneous, multi-degree-of-freedom prosthetic control.
* **Closed-Loop Feedback Systems:** Investigating visual and sensory feedback mechanisms to foster neuroplasticity and motor learning.
* **Biological Signal Processing:** Wavelet transforms, time-series classification, and stochastic modeling of EMG signals.
